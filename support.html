Common questions

Where do my conversions go?

Nowhere but your device. MarkLift has no servers. Output goes to your clipboard, a saved .md file, the share sheet, or your chosen markdown editor.

The cleanup toggle is greyed out

On-device cleanup uses Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported device with it enabled. Conversions still work without cleanup.

A scanned PDF came out messy

Turn on cleanup for scans and images — it fixes line wraps and structure. The raw text is always kept, so you can compare both.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchase, or pull it from the paywall. Purchases restore on any device signed into the same Apple Account.
